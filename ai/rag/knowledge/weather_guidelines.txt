WeatherGPT Decision Support Knowledge Base

GENERAL PRINCIPLES

WeatherGPT combines verified weather data with a deterministic Risk
Engine and Decision Engine.

The Risk Engine calculates weather impact levels using the available
weather variables.

The Decision Engine converts the calculated risk into an
activity-specific decision.

The calculated Risk Engine result and Decision Engine result are
authoritative.

Retrieved knowledge provides supporting context only. It must never
override a calculated risk level or decision.

RISK LEVELS

Minimal risk:
Weather-related impact is minimal. Normal precautions are generally
recommended.

Low risk:
Weather-related impact is relatively low. Normal precautions are
recommended.

Moderate risk:
Weather conditions may require additional precautions.

High risk:
Weather conditions may significantly affect outdoor activities.
Depending on the activity, caution or postponement may be appropriate.

Extreme risk:
Weather conditions represent the highest risk category in the
WeatherGPT decision model. Outdoor activities may need to be avoided
according to the calculated decision.

RUNNING

Running is a strenuous outdoor activity.

The Decision Engine should be treated as authoritative for running
decisions.

If the calculated decision is "proceed", the response may explain
that the calculated weather risk is relatively low.

If the calculated decision is "caution", the response should explain
the relevant weather impacts and recommend appropriate precautions.

If the calculated decision is "postpone", the response should explain
that postponement is the calculated recommendation.

If the calculated decision is "avoid", the response should explain
that avoiding strenuous outdoor activity is the calculated
recommendation.

CYCLING

Cycling is an outdoor physical activity that may be affected by heat,
rain, and wind.

The calculated Decision Engine result must be preserved.

High wind or high precipitation impact may affect cycling conditions
according to the WeatherGPT risk model.

RUNNING, CYCLING, AND SPORTS

Running, cycling, and outdoor sports can involve sustained physical
activity.

The language model must not independently change a calculated
decision.

For high or extreme calculated risk, the response should clearly
communicate the calculated recommendation.

WALKING

Walking is generally less strenuous than running or outdoor sports,
but weather conditions can still affect the activity.

The Decision Engine determines whether the calculated recommendation
is to proceed, use caution, or avoid the activity.

TRAVEL

Travel may be affected by precipitation and wind.

High rain impact may increase weather-related travel concerns.

High wind impact may increase weather-related travel concerns.

The Decision Engine's calculated travel decision is authoritative.

The language model must not independently declare travel safe or
unsafe when a calculated decision is available.

FARMING

Farming and agricultural activities can be affected by temperature,
precipitation, wind, and humidity.

High precipitation may affect weather-sensitive agricultural
activities.

High wind may affect exposed agricultural activities.

The Decision Engine's calculated farming recommendation must be
preserved.

HEAT

Temperature is one of the weather variables evaluated by the
WeatherGPT Risk Engine.

The project's current heat impact categories are:

Below 30 degrees Celsius:
low heat impact.

30 to below 35 degrees Celsius:
moderate heat impact.

35 to below 40 degrees Celsius:
high heat impact.

40 degrees Celsius or above:
extreme heat impact.

These thresholds are project-defined heuristics used by the
WeatherGPT Risk Engine. They are not official medical or
meteorological safety thresholds.

RAIN

Precipitation probability is evaluated by the WeatherGPT Risk Engine.

The project's current rain impact categories are:

Below 30 percent:
minimal rain impact.

30 to below 50 percent:
low rain impact.

50 to below 80 percent:
moderate rain impact.

80 percent or above:
high rain impact.

These thresholds are project-defined heuristics.

WIND

Wind speed is evaluated by the WeatherGPT Risk Engine.

The project's current wind impact categories are:

Below 15 km/h:
low wind impact.

15 to below 30 km/h:
moderate wind impact.

30 to below 50 km/h:
high wind impact.

50 km/h or above:
extreme wind impact.

These thresholds are project-defined heuristics.

HUMIDITY

Humidity is evaluated when humidity data is available.

The project's current humidity impact categories are:

Below 60 percent:
low humidity impact.

60 to below 80 percent:
moderate humidity impact.

80 percent or above:
high humidity impact.

These thresholds are project-defined heuristics.

WHAT-IF ANALYSIS

WeatherGPT supports hypothetical weather scenarios.

Examples include:

"What if the temperature reaches 40 degrees?"

"What if there is an 80 percent chance of rain?"

"What if wind speed reaches 50 km/h?"

"What if humidity reaches 90 percent?"

The What-If Engine calculates the hypothetical weather risk using the
same deterministic Risk Engine.

The Decision Engine then determines the activity-specific decision.

The language model must explain the calculated hypothetical result
without changing it.

FORECAST CONFIDENCE

WeatherGPT may provide a forecast confidence value.

Forecast confidence is a project-defined heuristic based on forecast
horizon and available uncertainty information.

It should be presented as a WeatherGPT confidence estimate and not
as an official probability of forecast correctness.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS

WeatherGPT's Risk Engine thresholds are software heuristics created
for the project.

They are not official government warnings, medical advice,
meteorological warnings, or emergency instructions.

For severe weather or emergency situations, users should consult
official local weather warnings and relevant local authorities.

RAG ROLE

RAG provides supporting domain knowledge.

RAG does not calculate weather risk.

RAG does not replace the Risk Engine.

RAG does not replace the Decision Engine.

RAG does not modify numerical weather data.

RAG does not override an authoritative calculated decision.

The final response should prioritize verified weather data and
calculated Risk Engine and Decision Engine results.
